## How the program works

Goal: lose body fat (belly/face especially) while keeping muscle, via three levers:

1. **Eat slightly less** — calorie deficit, target ≈ 1,750 kcal/day (removes fat).
2. **Eat enough protein** — target ≈ 160 g/day, spread across meals (protects muscle, keeps you full).
3. **Train 3× a week** — short gym sessions signal the body to keep muscle; cardio built into each session.

Expected result: ≈ 0.5 kg fat lost per week.

Note: "spot reduction" doesn't work — fat comes off the whole body in a deficit, belly/face are just where it's most visible.

### Glossary
- **Calorie deficit** — eating fewer calories than you burn.
- **Protein** — nutrient that builds/keeps muscle (chicken, beef, eggs, prawns, yogurt, whey).
- **Macros** — protein, carbs, fat (mainly track calories + protein).
- **Rep** — one full movement.
- **Set** — group of reps done back-to-back ("3×10" = 3 sets of 10 reps).
- **RPE / reps in reserve** — stop ~2 reps before failure.
- **Machine vs DB** — DB = dumbbell; each exercise has a machine version and dumbbell backup.
- **Deload** — planned easier week for recovery.

## Part 1 — Nutrition

### Daily targets

| What | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Calories | ~1,750 kcal | Below ~2,300 maintenance → steady fat loss |
| Protein | 150–170 g (aim 160) | Protects muscle, keeps you full |
| Carbs | ~140–160 g | Energy for training; rice & potatoes fine |
| Fat | ~55–65 g | Needed for hormones & health |
| Water | 2.5–3 L | Controls hunger; reduces face puffiness |

**Only two numbers really matter:** stay near 1,750 kcal and hit 150+ g protein. Never drop below ~1,600 kcal (near baseline burn).

**Day formula:** Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner + 1–2 snacks ≈ 1,750 kcal / ~160 g protein. Pick one option per section, mix and match freely.

### Breakfast (pick one)
Milk = semi-skimmed. Whey = chocolate. Greek yogurt = 0% / high-protein.

**Chocolate–Banana smoothie** — ~440 kcal · 49 g P
- Chocolate whey: 1 scoop (~30 g)
- Milk: 250 ml
- Banana: 120 g
- Greek yogurt (recommended): 150 g
- Blend 2 min.

**Chocolate–Blueberry smoothie** — ~420 kcal · 48 g P
- Chocolate whey: 1 scoop (~30 g)
- Milk: 250 ml
- Blueberries: 100 g
- Greek yogurt (recommended): 150 g
- Blend 2 min.

**Overnight oats** (mix in a jar at night, eat cold in the morning)

| Ingredient | Banana version | Blueberry version |
|---|---|---|
| Oats (dry) | 40 g | 40 g |
| Chocolate whey | 1 scoop | 1 scoop |
| Milk | 200 ml | 200 ml |
| Fruit | Banana 120 g | Blueberries 100 g |
| + Greek yogurt (optional) | 100 g | 100 g |
| + Cashews (optional) | 15 g | 15 g |
| **With yogurt + cashews** | **~620 kcal · 51 g P** | **~575 kcal · 50 g P** |

Beginner tip: stir the whey into the milk *first* until smooth, then add oats and fruit — won't clump.

**Quick alternatives (no blender):**
- Greek yogurt bowl — 200 g yogurt + 100 g blueberries + 20 g cashews + cinnamon → ~330 kcal · 25 g P
- Eggs — 3 eggs (scrambled or air-fried) + 1 slice wholegrain toast + tomato → ~320 kcal · 22 g P

### Lunch — cook & prep (batch on Sunday)
All weights raw/before cooking. Weigh on a kitchen scale, cook, split into containers. Each recipe = one portion — multiply ×4–6 to batch a week. Keeps 3–4 days fridge, freeze rest.

**R1 · Chicken, rice & broccoli** — ~530 kcal · 54 g P
- Chicken breast 200 g, White rice (dry) 60 g, Broccoli 150 g, Olive oil 5 g
- Air-fry chicken 190°C, 18–22 min. Boil rice. Steam/air-fry broccoli 200°C, 10–12 min.

**R2 · Lean beef & potato** — ~490 kcal · 40 g P
- Lean beef mince (5% fat) 150 g, Potato 250 g, Broccoli/green beans 150 g, Olive oil 5 g
- Pan-fry mince (no oil — fat renders) with spices. Air-fry potato cubes 200°C, 20 min. Steam veg.

**R3 · Chicken rice porridge (congee)** — ~410 kcal · 38 g P
- Chicken breast 150 g, White rice (dry) 50 g, Water/stock ~600 ml, Carrot 80 g, Ginger/garlic/spring onion to taste
- One pot: simmer rice + chicken + stock + carrot 30–40 min until porridge-like; shred chicken back in. Reheats great — add water.

**R4 · Garlic prawns, rice & broccoli** — ~460 kcal · 40 g P
- Prawns (frozen, peeled) 180 g, White rice (dry) 60 g, Broccoli/green beans 150 g, Olive oil + garlic + chili 5 g
- Prawns air-fry from frozen 200°C, 6–8 min. Fastest recipe here.

No prep day? Grab a ready grilled-chicken tray + microwave rice pouch + bag of salad. Pick trays with ≥25 g protein and ≤550 kcal on the label.

### Dinner (pick one) — lighter than lunch, most need little/no cooking. Any lunch recipe works here too. All weights raw, each = one portion.

**D1 · Beef or chicken stir-fry** — ~450 kcal · 40 g P
- Chicken or 5% beef 150 g, Frozen stir-fry veg 200 g, White rice (dry) 40 g, Oil + garlic/ginger/soy 5 g
- Pan or air-fry, high heat, 8–10 min.

**D2 · Prawns & veg (low-carb)** — ~300 kcal · 35 g P
- Prawns (frozen, peeled) 180 g, Salad/mixed veg 200 g, Olive oil + garlic + lemon 5 g
- Prawns air-fry from frozen 200°C, 6–8 min.

**D3 · Egg & chicken salad** — ~465 kcal · 51 g P
- Eggs 3 (150 g), Cold cooked chicken 100 g, Tomato + cucumber 200 g, Olive oil 5 g
- No cooking — boil/air-fry eggs in a batch.

**D4 · Chickpea bowl** — ~460 kcal · 42 g P
- Chickpeas (1 tin, drained) 240 g, Prawns or cold chicken 100 g, Veg + onion 150 g, Olive oil + lemon 5 g
- No cooking if using pre-cooked protein.

**D5 · Omelette** — ~355 kcal · 27 g P
- Eggs 4 (200 g), Tomato/onion + side salad 150 g, Olive oil 3 g
- Pan 5–6 min.

**D6 · Greek yogurt bowl (light night)** — ~315 kcal · 29 g P
- Greek yogurt (0%) 250 g, Berries 100 g, Cashews 20 g
- No cooking.

### Golden rules
- Protein at every meal.
- Volume beats hunger — pile on veg & yogurt.
- Liquid calories (juice, sugary coffee, alcohol) quietly wreck a deficit — keep alcohol rare.
- Weigh yourself 3×/week, same time; judge the weekly average (daily swings of 1–2 kg are just water).
- One flexible meal a week — enjoy it, then back to plan.
- Less salt + alcohol + more water de-puffs the face fast.

**If the scale stalls 10+ days:**
1. Recount 3 days honestly — cashews, oil and ready-meal calories are the usual hidden culprits.
2. Add your weekend walk *before* cutting food.
3. Only then trim ~100–150 kcal (smaller rice/potato). Never below ~1,600 kcal.

## Part 2 — Training

Three short sessions a week. Machines are the backbone — safe, easy to learn, allow pushing hard without a spotter. Each workout ≈ 50 minutes including cardio.

### Weekly schedule

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Push — chest, shoulders, triceps |
| Tue | Rest (optional 8-min home core) |
| Wed | Pull — back, biceps, rear shoulders |
| Thu | Rest (optional 8-min home core) |
| Fri | Legs — quads, hamstrings, glutes |
| Weekend | One easy 30–60 min walk |

### How a session flows

| Stage | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 · Warm-up | Treadmill: incline 10, speed 5.0, 10 min |
| 2 · Weights | The 5 exercises for the day (below). Straight sets. |
| 3 · Finish | 10–15 min steady spin bike or rower |

**Straight sets:** do all sets of one exercise, resting ~60–75 seconds between them, then move to next exercise. Push each set until it feels hard — stop about 2 reps before failure.

**Reading the tables:** use the Machine version normally. If taken, do the Dumbbell swap instead (trains same muscle). "3 × 10–12" = 3 sets of 10 to 12 reps.

### Cardio

| When | Do | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Incline 10, speed 5.0 — 10 min | Warm-up + high-intensity fat burn |
| Close | Spin or row, steady — 10–15 min | Extra calorie burn, easy on joints |

(Core work is for posture/strength, not belly-fat burning — that's the diet's job.)

### Progression

**The one rule of progress:** when you can hit the top of the rep range on all sets with clean form, add a little weight next time (~2.5 kg on machines/bars, 1–2 kg on dumbbells) and drop back to the bottom of the range. Write down weight × reps every session — numbers going up means it's working, even on days the scale won't move.

Take a deload occasionally (same weights, drop the last exercise, ease off cardio) whenever you feel run down, then go back to pushing harder.

### Staying safe
Form before weight — watch one short technique video per lift before your first try. Machines are safe to push to your limit alone. **Sharp or joint pain = stop and swap the exercise; muscle burn is normal.** Take rest days — muscle grows during recovery, not in the gym. If you ever feel chest pain or dizziness during hard cardio, stop and get checked.
